# stock_traceability_report

## Source system
The table likely originates from an Odoo ERP system. The naming convention of columns such as `create_uid`, `write_uid`, `create_date`, and `write_date`, combined with the use of a `nextval` sequence on the `id` column, is highly characteristic of the Odoo framework's internal audit and tracking pattern.

## Functional process 
This table supports the inventory and supply chain management process, specifically focusing on the audit trail of stock movements or traceability events. It tracks which users created or modified specific traceability records and when those actions occurred, ensuring accountability within the warehouse management lifecycle.
