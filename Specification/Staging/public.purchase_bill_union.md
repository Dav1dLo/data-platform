## Source system
The table likely originates from an ERP system such as Odoo or a similar modular business management suite. The naming convention (e.g., `partner_id`, `company_id`, `vendor_bill_id`) and the structure of linking purchase orders to bills are characteristic of integrated accounting and procurement modules.

## Functional process 
This table supports the Procure-to-Pay (P2P) process, specifically tracking the reconciliation and recording of vendor invoices against purchase orders. It acts as a unified staging point for financial obligations, capturing the relationship between entities (partners), companies, and specific procurement documents.

## Description
One row in this table represents a single purchase bill or invoice record, capturing the financial obligation and its association with a vendor and a purchase order. As a staging table, it serves as a raw, landed copy of transactional data intended for further transformation into downstream analytical models.
